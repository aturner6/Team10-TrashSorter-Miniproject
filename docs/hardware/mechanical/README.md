## From Concept to CAD: SolidWorks Implementation
  <br> <br>
Using the dimensions and concepts mapped out in the initial sketches, the final components were modeled in SolidWorks. The isometric views below show the exact geometry used to 3D print the enclosure, incorporating the required motor mounts and external USB-C pathways.

<br>

<img width="1569" height="1101" alt="Screenshot 2026-09-15 152336" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/86fa68aa-47ae-4992-84da-4fb064e89734" />
<br>
<br>
This component serves as the enclosure lid and the timer's primary display surface, featuring a central clearance hole for the motor shaft and the indicator assembly.
<br>
<br>
</div>

<div align= "center">

As shown in Figures A and B, these parts serve as the physical interface for the timer. This component acts as the outer structural attachment for the tactile button. It mounts to the main enclosure and provides a precise  guide for the button stud to travel through smoothly.
<br>
<br>
</div>

<div align="center">
<img width="1401" height="1009" alt="Screenshot 2026-09-15 152119" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6e2612c6-9130-41ee-b6dc-9e6957c54b55" />
<br>
<br>
This bracket secures the stepper motor in place. Its side tabs allow it to be fastened firmly to the enclosure, fulfilling the required physical motor mount specification.
<br>
<br>
</div>

<div align="center">
<img width="601" height="1164" alt="Screenshot 2026-09-15 152056" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/38db9c4d-a5c8-4939-bbdb-da848bc4b360" />
<br>
<br>
The cylindrical sleeve features an internal keyed geometry design to mate with the flat edges of the stepper motor's shaft, ensuring the rotational torque is transferred to the clock hand without slipping.
<br>
<br>
</div>


<div align="center">
<img width="615" height="1142" alt="Screenshot 2026-09-15 152404" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/4f80a523-8d58-4311-b73c-2a66953a7b95" />
<br>
<br>
This is the physical plunger that the user presses. It rests inside the housing and makes direct contact with the tactile buttons on the board to input the 15, 20, 25, or 30-minute timer preset.
